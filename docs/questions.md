# Questions and Choices in the Quiz

All questions, choices and answers shall be listed here.
For each question, four choices are offered, and one of them is correct, which is indicated in bold.

For each section, a difficulty level shall be assigned and shall increase as the player progresses through the quiz. A section can have 12 questions, totalling up to 60 questions.
